Every player has a **notebook** for each mission. The **📒** button on the side of the game opens it: a spiral-bound book whose cover swings open, with the contents on the left page and the page being written on the right. On a phone, one page shows at a time and the **📑** button shows the contents.

The notes belong to **that mission only**. Another mission has a notebook of its own, and so does the same mission in another class's game.

## What students can do

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Pages** | Write on lined paper. Each page has a name, a picture beside its name (so a page can be found without reading) and a colour, which is also its tab on the edge of the book |
| **Subpages** | A page can hold subpages, and those can hold their own: three levels in all. The contents lists each page with its subpages under it |
| **Sticky notes** | Short notes stuck on the page |
| **Flip cards** | A question on the front and the answer on the back, for practising: **Flip** turns the card over |
| **To-dos** | Something to check off |
| **Organising** | Drag a page in the contents to move it: drop it above or below another page, or onto the middle of a page to make it a subpage. ⚙️ on a page moves it up or down, puts it under the page above or takes it out again, changes its colour or throws it away (it asks first). Cards can be dragged into a new order, or onto a page in the contents to move them there; ⚙️ on a card does the same with buttons |

Each page and card has a small note saying when it was last written: "just now", "today at 2:41 PM", "yesterday at 9:05 AM", "on Tuesday".

A notebook holds up to 50 pages, each with up to 5,000 letters and 30 cards.

## Easy to read

The writing is in **Neat writing** (Playpen Sans), a handwriting designed for children who are learning to read: clear letter shapes, a single-storey *a*, and lots of space. The **Aa** button lets each student choose **Quick writing** (a looser handwriting) or **Book letters** (plain print), and **Big** or **Bigger** text. That choice is kept on their device, for every mission.

## What is kept, and who sees it

- What a student writes is kept as they write, a moment after they stop typing, and again when they close the notebook or leave the page. "✓ Saved" at the foot of the contents says it is kept; if the connection drops, it says so and tries again.
- **Only the student writes in their notebook.** In a mission you assigned, you can **read** it on the Classes page (see [Reading your students' notebooks](#reading-your-students-notebooks)); the notebook's cover and contents tell the student "👀 *your name* can read this notebook." In a public game nobody else sees it, not even you.
- It is kept **with the student's save**. [Resetting a student's progress](/docs/editor/reports/#starting-a-student-again) empties their notebook for that game, and deleting a student deletes all of theirs. A records request ([`/api/v1` export](/docs/api-v1/)) includes it.
- Guests have a notebook too, for as long as their guest account lasts.
- When you **test** a scene from the editor, the notebook works but nothing in it is kept.

## Reading your students' notebooks

On the **Classes** page, each mission you assigned has a **📒 Notebooks** button beside **📊 Report**. It lists the students in that assignment with their team, how many pages and cards they have written, and when they last wrote. Choose a student to read their notebook: the contents first, then every page in order, with each subpage marked with the page it sits under. Flip cards show their front and back together, and to-dos show whether they are checked off.

- It is **read only**: you cannot change what a student wrote.
- You see the notebook each student keeps **in this assignment's game** (their team's, if they are in one). Notes they keep in a public game, or in another class's game, are not shown.
- An admin can read the notebooks in any assignment, as with reports.

## Turning it off

The notebook is on in every mission. To turn it off, open **Settings**, find **💬 Talking and reading**, and untick **Players have a notebook**. The 📒 button no longer shows; what players wrote is kept, and shows again if you turn it back on.
